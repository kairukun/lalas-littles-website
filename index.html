About our center

A safe place to explore, learn, and belong.

LaLa's Littles Childcare & Preschool has been caring for Corsicana families since 2013. Our licensed center welcomes infants through school-age children with attentive educators, playful learning, and a secure environment parents can trust.

We are accepting new enrollments and happy to help you find the right fit — whether you need full-day care, preschool readiness, or before-and-after school support.
