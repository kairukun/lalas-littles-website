About our center

A safe place to explore, learn, and belong.

LaLa's Littles is a childcare and preschool for kids ages 6 weeks to school-aged. Our licensed Corsicana center has been caring for families since 2013 with attentive educators, playful learning, and a secure environment parents can trust.

We are accepting new enrollments and happy to help you find the right fit — whether you need full-day care, preschool readiness, or before-and-after school support.
